01 / RETRIES

A retry is more traffic.

When a service is overloaded, asking again can make it slower. Backoff buys time; jitter keeps clients from asking together.

02 / TIMEOUTS

Giving up ≠ stopping work.

A client can stop waiting while its request still occupies a worker. Watch the faded slots: that is work nobody is waiting for.

03 / TRADEOFFS

There is no magic toggle.

A breaker protects a dependency by refusing calls. It can also reject useful work. Compare successful, failed, and unfinished clients together.
